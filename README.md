# MathCraft: Addition, Subtraction & Multiplication Academy

**MathCraft** is a visual math learning web app engineered specifically to teach **Addition, Subtraction, and Multiplication** through interactive manipulatives, step-by-step regrouping solvers, mental math strategies, a 60-second speed blitz, and real-world story word problems.

---

## 🌟 Modules & Learning Tools

### 1. ➕ Addition Masterclass
* **Ten-Frames ("Make a 10" Strategy):** Helps young learners see how numbers decompose. For example, when adding $8 + 5$, Frame 1 takes 2 from the 5 to form a full 10, leaving 3 in Frame 2. ($8 + 5 = 10 + 3 = 13$).
* **Base-10 Blocks Lab:** Explores place value using Hundreds flats, Tens rods, and Ones unit cubes with real-time expanded notation ($100 + 30 + 6 = 136$).
* **Step-by-Step Column Addition Solver:** Interactive vertical math stepper showing carrying (`+1` red bubbles) to the Tens column.

### 2. ➖ Subtraction Masterclass
* **Number Line Distance & Jumps:** Visualizes subtraction as backward hops from a starting number with quadratic jump arcs and markers.
* **Interactive Fact Family Triangle:** Teaches that addition and subtraction are inverse operations. Connects 3 numbers in 4 equations (e.g. $7+5=12$, $5+7=12$, $12-5=7$, $12-7=5$).
* **Column Subtraction with Borrowing (Regrouping):** Interactive stepper showing how borrowing 1 Ten changes the Tens digit (e.g. $7 \to 6$) and gives 10 Ones (e.g. $2 \to 12$).

### 3. ✖️ Multiplication Masterclass
* **Rectangular Array & Commutative Property:** Adjust rows and columns up to $10 \times 10$. Test commutative symmetry ($4 \times 6 \leftrightarrow 6 \times 4 = 24$) with repeated addition breakdowns ($6 + 6 + 6 + 6 = 24$).
* **12×12 Times Table Matrix:** Interactive factor explorer highlighting rows, columns, and square numbers along the diagonal ($x^2$).
* **2-Digit Area Box (Window) Model:** Splits 2-digit multiplication into Tens and Ones (e.g., $24 \times 13 = (20+4) \times (10+3)$) and computes the 4 colored sub-areas ($200 + 40 + 60 + 12 = 312$).

### 4. ⚡ 60-Second Math Fluency Blitz
* Rapid-fire game mode with timer, combo multipliers (x1 up to x5), sound effects, and high score tracking.
* Choose between **Addition Blitz**, **Subtraction Blitz**, **Multiplication Blitz**, or **Mixed Blitz**.
* Awards ranks: *Bronze Thinker*, *Silver Solver*, *Gold Mathlete*, *Diamond Prodigy*.

### 5. 🎯 Practice & Story Word Problems
* Infinite procedural question generation across Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Real-world Story Word Problems.
* Dynamic hint button explaining the problem-solving strategy and identifying story keywords ("in total", "leftover", "each contains").
* Confetti animations, Web Audio synthesis chords, XP points, daily streak, and mastery badges.
